OPERATOR PRECEDENCE

Definition:

Operator precedence determines the order in which operators are
evaluated in an expression.

Operators with higher precedence are evaluated first.


Common Operator Precedence:

    Priority    Operators              Meaning
    ------------------------------------------------
    1           ()                     Parentheses
    2           *, /, %                Multiplication, Division, Modulus
    3           +, -                   Addition, Subtraction
    4           <, >, <=, >=           Comparison
    5           ==, !=                 Equality
    6           &&                     Logical AND
    7           ||                     Logical OR
    8           =, +=, -=, *=, /=      Assignment


Example:

    int result = 10 + 5 * 2;

    First:
    5 * 2 = 10

    Then:
    10 + 10 = 20


Output:

    20


Why?

    * has higher precedence than +.


Example with parentheses:

    int result = (10 + 5) * 2;


Output:

    30


Here:

    (10 + 5) is evaluated first because
    parentheses have the highest precedence.


Key Points:

    1. Operator precedence decides the order of evaluation.
    2. Higher precedence operators are evaluated first.
    3. Parentheses () have the highest priority in normal expressions.
    4. If operators have the same precedence, associativity decides
       the order of evaluation.
    5. Parentheses can be used to change the order of evaluation.


In simple words:

    Operator Precedence = kaunsa operator pehle calculate hoga,
    ye decide karta hai.


Exam Answer:

Operator precedence is the rule that determines the order in which
operators are evaluated in an expression. Operators with higher
precedence are evaluated before operators with lower precedence.
