BITWISE OPERATORS

Definition:

Bitwise operators are used to perform operations on the individual
bits of integer values.


Bitwise Operators:

    Operator    Meaning             Example
    ------------------------------------------------
    &           Bitwise AND         a & b
    |           Bitwise OR          a | b
    ^           Bitwise XOR         a ^ b
    ~           Bitwise NOT         ~a
    <<          Left Shift          a << 1
    >>          Right Shift         a >> 1


Example:

    int a = 5;
    int b = 3;

    cout << (a & b);


Binary values:

    a = 5  → 0101
    b = 3  → 0011

    0101
    0011
    ----
    0001

    Output:
    1


Here:

    &  = AND operation on bits
    |  = OR operation on bits
    ^  = XOR operation on bits
    ~  = reverses the bits
    << = shifts bits to the left
    >> = shifts bits to the right


Important:

    5 & 3 = 1
    5 | 3 = 7
    5 ^ 3 = 6


Key Points:

    1. Bitwise operators work on individual bits.
    2. They are mainly used with integer data types.
    3. & means Bitwise AND.
    4. | means Bitwise OR.
    5. ^ means Bitwise XOR.
    6. ~ means Bitwise NOT.
    7. << and >> are shift operators.
